Cookie Policy

How cookies and similar technologies are used on Loopnod.

Effective Date: August 19, 2026 · Version: 2026-08-19

This Cookie Policy explains how Francis&M_Tech ("Loopnod," "we," "us") uses cookies and similar technologies, and applies alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. The Mobile App

The Loopnod mobile app does not use browser cookies — cookies are a web-browser technology. Instead, the app stores a small amount of data directly on your device (for example, your login session and, for encrypted messaging, the private keys described in our Privacy Policy) using your device's standard app storage, not a cookie. This on-device storage is covered by our Privacy Policy, not this Cookie Policy.

2. Loopnod Websites

If you visit a Loopnod website — for example, one hosting these policies, or a marketing site — we use a limited set of cookies:

Category Purpose Can you opt out?
Strictly necessary Keep the site functioning: page navigation, security, remembering your cookie preference itself No — required for the site to work
Functional Remember settings like your chosen theme or language, where offered Yes, via your browser or an in-page control if we offer one
Analytics Understand aggregate site usage (e.g., which pages are visited) — only if and where we've enabled an analytics tool Yes, where offered
Advertising Track you across sites for ad targeting Not currently used. We don't run advertising cookies on Loopnod websites. See our Advertising Policy.

We do not currently use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies on any Loopnod website.

3. Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking strictly-necessary cookies may prevent parts of a Loopnod website from working correctly. Where we use functional or analytics cookies, we'll provide an in-page way to manage your preference for visitors in the EU/UK/EEA and other jurisdictions that require it.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our websites and their cookie use change. Material changes will be reflected in the Effective Date above.
