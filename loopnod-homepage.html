Five ways to start a loop.

Pick a format, add a caption or a sound, and it's out — no separate app for each kind of post.

Photo

Share a moment straight from your gallery or camera.

Video

Post a clip with a sound playing behind it, or none at all.

Text

Say it plainly, in a loop built just for words.

Poll

Ask your circle a question and watch the votes come in.

Audio

Drop a voice note or a sound clip on its own.

Sound

Loops carry sound, not just captions.

Attach a track to a loop, browse what's picked for you, or upload a full release. Sound sits inside the same feed as everything else — nothing to switch apps for.

  • Upload a track, an album, or a live session
  • A "Picked for you" mix that updates through the day
  • Pin a sound to any photo or video loop
Messages

Keep the conversation with your circle.

Message one to one or start a group for the people you loop with most. Everything stays attached to the same account as your posts and sounds.

  • Direct messages alongside group threads
  • Start a group in a couple of taps
  • Leave a note on someone's loop without leaving the app
Privacy & security

What happens in a chat, stays in that chat.

Nothing here is on by default until you choose it. Set messages to disappear, block screenshots, and decide chat by chat whether read receipts even exist.

  • Disappearing messages — off, 24 hours, 7 days, or 90 days
  • Screenshot protection blocks screenshots and screen recording, for both of you
  • Read receipts on your terms, set per chat
Pulse

Every pulse, follow, and note — in one place.

Pulse is the heartbeat of your account: who followed you, who pulsed your loop, who left a note on your status. One tab, not four different menus to check.

  • See every pulse and follow the moment it happens
  • Know exactly which loop or status got the reaction
  • Nothing to refresh — it's just there when you open the app
Appearance

Make it yours — down to the surface.

Switch between Pearl Aurora and Obsidian Glow, then choose how every card feels: soft neumorphic surfaces, frosted glass, or flat and plain. This page borrows the same idea — plush, textured, and built from the app's own materials.

  • Two full appearances, light and dark
  • Neumorphism, glassmorphism, or normal cards
  • Text size and autoplay, set once and forget
