Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Effective Date: September 6, 2026 · Version: 2026-09-06

Age assurance (see Section 7) is handled through self-attestation and does not involve uploading an ID or a photo of your face to our servers.

1.5 Direct message and Group message privacy

What we cannot see: The content of your direct messages and Group messages — text, voice note audio, and shared audio clip audio — is protected by end-to-end encryption before it ever leaves your device. Our servers store and relay only encrypted ciphertext. We do not have the cryptographic keys needed to decrypt it, and as a matter of engineering design, not just policy, we cannot read your message content — including in response to our own curiosity, an internal request, or most legal process. This is true for Group messages exactly as it is for direct messages.

What we can see: Message and Group metadata is not encrypted and is visible to us as the operator of the Service: which accounts are in a conversation, which Groups exist and who's in them with what role, when messages were sent, and — for voice notes and shared audio clips — the approximate size and duration of the file (not its content). We also process your public identity keys (used to set up encrypted sessions) and, transiently, encrypted file blobs in cloud storage, access to which is restricted to conversation or Group participants.

Beta status. The Service, including this encryption, is currently offered as a beta. Our encryption is our own implementation of the Double Ratchet and X3DH protocols (the same publicly documented protocols Signal and WhatsApp use), built on standard cryptographic primitives rather than on Signal's own libsignal library, and it has not yet completed an independent third-party security audit. See Terms of Service Section 7.1 for detail. Treat it as a strong, good-faith protection during this period rather than an independently verified guarantee.

Anyone in a Group can read what's sent to it, for as long as they're a member. Encryption protects Group content from us and from outsiders — it does not, and cannot, protect you from another member of a Group you joined or were added to. See our Terms of Service for how adding someone to a Group works, and your options if you're added somewhere you don't want to be.

By default, there is no message backup or account recovery for encrypted content, for either direct messages or Group messages. The private keys that decrypt your messages live only on your device(s), and we do not build any server-side mechanism to back them up, escrow them, or recover them, because doing so would create a place a compromise or legal order could expose content we otherwise cannot see.

Chat backups. You can optionally turn on chat backup (Settings → Chats → Chat backup). When you do, your backup is saved to your own Google Drive — storage tied to your Google account, not to Loopnod — and we do not receive, store, or keep any copy of it on our side. If you choose an end-to-end encrypted backup, it's protected by a password or key that only you create and hold; we never receive, see, log, or store it, and we have no way to recover your backup if you lose it — including if you ask us to. A standard backup is protected using your account sign-in instead, so there's no separate password to lose, but it also means anyone able to access your account can access that backup. You can turn chat backup off or delete a backup at any time, from the app or directly in your Google Drive; because we never held a copy, there's nothing on our end left to delete.

Linking a device. Adding a device (Settings → Linked Devices) can now bring over your past conversation history from one of your other active devices, in addition to future messages — this happens directly between your own devices and stays end-to-end encrypted the whole way; we relay ciphertext we cannot read, the same as any other message. If you sign out, uninstall the app, or lose every device your keys were on — with no other linked device to sync from and no chat backup turned on — the messages that were only there are gone, including to us.

If another user has your contact information and connects it with our Service, or if another user messages you, mentions you, or adds you to a Group, we may receive limited information about you from them.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • create and maintain your account, and authenticate you;
  • operate the Service's core features (posting, messaging, Groups, following, etc.);
  • route and deliver end-to-end encrypted messages, without being able to read their content;
  • detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service and Community Standards, including automated detection tools (for example, checking a publicly uploaded music track against known copyrighted recordings, or scanning publicly posted content for apparent CSAM) — these tools never operate on end-to-end encrypted message content, see Section 1.5;
  • provide optional features you choose to use, such as translating a Loop's caption into another language;
  • diagnose and fix crashes and technical errors, using automatically stripped, message-content-free diagnostic data where crash reporting is enabled for your build — see Section 1.2;
  • respond to your support requests;
  • send you service-related communications (security alerts, verification codes) and, where you've opted in, product updates;
  • comply with legal obligations, including responding to valid legal process and CSAM reporting obligations;
  • improve and develop the Service, including through aggregated or de-identified analysis of usage patterns.

We do not use the content of your direct or Group messages for any purpose, including the ones above, because we are not able to — see Section 1.5.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (EU/UK/EEA Users)

If you're located in the EU, UK, or EEA, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the Service you've signed up for (account operation, posting, messaging).
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and improve our features, balanced against your rights and interests.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable law, including CSAM reporting obligations.
  • Consent — for optional communications like marketing emails, which you can withdraw at any time.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Service under contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations, currently including Supabase (backend/database), the Expo push notification service (which relies on Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging), Cloudflare (for optional caption translation and for automated scanning of publicly posted content and publicly uploaded tracks — never your message content), and, where enabled for a given build, Sentry (crash and error monitoring, with message content and credentials stripped before anything leaves your device — see Section 1.2);
  • Legal process — where required to comply with a valid subpoena, warrant, court order, or other legal obligation, to the extent our end-to-end encryption architecture technically allows us to comply. Where legally permitted, we notify the affected user before disclosing account information in response to a government or legal request; we do not give notice where a court order or law prohibits it, or where doing so would create a safety risk (for example, a CSAM investigation or a credible threat of imminent harm);
  • Safety and enforcement — with law enforcement where necessary to investigate fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms, including mandatory CSAM reporting to NCMEC;
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy continuing to apply to your information;
  • With your consent — for any other purpose we've separately disclosed and you've agreed to.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When you delete a piece of content or your account, we remove it from active systems and complete removal from our own internal system backups and caches within a reasonable period, except where retention is legally required (for example, records related to a CSAM report). Message metadata is retained separately from message content, consistent with Section 1.5.

Chat backups you turn on are different. These live in your own Google Drive, not in any backup or cache of ours, so this Section doesn't apply to them — we have nothing to retain or delete on our side in the first place. Managing and deleting them is entirely in your hands: from the app (Settings → Chats → Chat backup) or directly in your Google Drive. For an end-to-end encrypted backup specifically, we also never hold the password or key protecting it, so we cannot open, restore, or recover one on your behalf under any circumstance, including a legal request — see Section 1.5.

If you're a resident of another state with a comprehensive privacy law, you may have similar rights to those described in Section 6.4, which we honor consistent with the applicable law of your state of residence.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Global minimum age is 13. Country-specific higher minimums apply where local social-media rules require them (Australia, Indonesia, and Malaysia: 16; France: 15). Other countries keep the global floor of 13.

Age assurance. For restricted countries (and under-16 signups in Brazil), signup includes an extra confirmation step. If we learn that we've collected personal information from a child under the applicable minimum age without required consent, we delete that information and terminate the associated account as quickly as practicable. Contact loopnod.support@gmail.com if you believe an under-minimum-age child has created an account.

7.1 Teen accounts (13–17) & parental controls

For account holders between 13 and 17, we apply additional default protections — see Terms of Service Section 1.4 for the full list (private account, restricted messaging, sensitive-content filtering, and a daily time limit with break reminders).

If a teen invites a parent or guardian and that person accepts, we link the two accounts and share the teen's daily screen time and their current time limit, break reminder, and messaging-permission settings with the guardian, who can then adjust them, including whether the teen's account is public or private. A linked guardian cannot see the teen's private messages, Loops, or Notes through this link, and cannot turn off sensitive-content filtering — that protection stays on regardless of what anyone sets. The teen can always switch their own account back to private; only the linked guardian can make it public again. A teen or their linked guardian can end the link at any time from Settings, which resets the account's safety settings back to their protective defaults and notifies the guardian.

8. Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

  • end-to-end encryption for direct message and Group message content, as described in Section 1.5;
  • encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest for account and content data;
  • database-level access controls (Row Level Security) restricting each user's data access to their own account and content they're entitled to see, enforced by our database, not just our application code;
  • restricted, audited access to production systems for our own personnel.

No system is completely secure. If we experience a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and relevant regulators as required by applicable law.
