Help Center

Guided articles for using Loopnod's features and troubleshooting issues.

Guided articles for getting the most out of Loopnod, and for staying safe while you do.

Have a question or ran into an issue?

Search the topics below, or skip straight to our Frequently Asked Questions. If you still can't find an answer, send us your question or issue directly — see Contact & Support at the bottom of this page, or email loopnod.support@gmail.com any time.

Getting Started

Creating an account

Download Loopnod, tap Sign Up, and enter your email or phone number, a password, your country, and your age. Your country and age determine which age-related protections apply to your account — see "Teen accounts & Family Center" below.

Completing your profile

After signup, add a display name, username, bio, and avatar photo from Complete Profile. You can change your username once every 30 days from Settings → Edit Profile.

Verification

Apply for a verified badge from Settings → Apply for Verification if you're a notable individual, creator, or organization. Review typically covers authenticity of the account and identity, not follower count.

Posting & Sharing

Posting a Loop

Tap the Create button to share a Loop — a photo, video, text post, poll, or audio clip. Loops appear in your followers' feeds in reverse-chronological order; we don't use an engagement-ranking algorithm to reorder what people see.

Commenting with Notes

Tap the comment icon on any Loop to leave a Note. You can delete your own Notes at any time, and Loop authors can remove Notes on their own posts.

Posting a Status

Status posts are short-lived updates visible to your followers (or approved followers only, if your account is private). Tap the Status tab to create one, and Status Style to customize its appearance. You can see who's viewed your Status from Status Viewers.

Resharing a Loop with Echo

Tap the Echo icon on a Loop to reshare it to your own followers. The original post appears nested inside your Echo, with your name and the time you shared it shown above it, so your followers can always see who originally posted it.

Messaging & Groups

Starting a direct message

Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted — text, voice notes, and shared audio clips. Open Messages, tap Compose, and pick a recipient. Because of how end-to-end encryption works, your messages live on your own devices, not ours. Turn on chat backup or link an additional device (see below) if you want a way to keep your history if something happens to your only device — without either, losing every device your keys were on means that conversation history is gone for good, including for us.

Backing up your chats

From Settings → Chats → Chat backup, you can save your messages to your own Google Drive. Choose:

  • End-to-end encrypted backup — set a backup password or key yourself, and keep it somewhere safe. We never see or store it, so we cannot recover it or your backup if it's lost.
  • Standard backup — protected by your account sign-in instead, so there's no extra password to remember.

Pick how often backups run, and turn chat backup off or delete a backup at any time — from the app or directly in your Google Drive.

Restoring your chats

On a new or reset device, choose Recover Messages during setup (or Settings → Chats → Recover Messages) and pick a backup to restore from. Recovered messages open in a separate, read-only Recovered Messages view, so they don't get mixed into your current conversations.

Customizing your chat wallpaper

Open a chat or Group, tap the contact/Group name, and choose Wallpaper to set a background for that conversation. This is a display setting stored on your device — it doesn't change what's encrypted or sent to other participants.

Verifying a safety number

For a sensitive conversation, open the chat, tap the contact's name, and select Safety Number to confirm you're talking to the device you think you are. This protects against a compromised key exchange that encryption alone can't catch.

Creating and managing a Group

Tap Groups → New Group, name it, and add members. As the creator, you're the first admin. From Group Info you can:

  • add or remove members (admins only);
  • promote or demote members to/from admin;
  • turn on "Only admins can send" to restrict posting;
  • leave the Group at any time, whatever your role.

Group messages are end-to-end encrypted the same way direct messages are — we can't read their content. Anyone currently in the Group can read what's sent to it, so only add people you trust with that.

Leaving or reporting a Group

Open Group Info and tap Leave Group at the bottom. If you're being harassed by a specific member, block them from their profile and report the behavior to us at loopnod.support@gmail.com with as much detail as you can share — since Group content is encrypted, a direct report from you is what lets us look into it.

Privacy & Safety

Blocking and muting

Open a user's profile → the "…" menu → Block or Mute. Blocking prevents them from following you, messaging you, or adding you to new Groups going forward; muting hides their content from your feed without notifying them.

Reporting content

Long-press any Loop, Note, Status, or direct message (from someone else) and tap Report. Reports are reviewed against our Community Standards.

Restricted messaging (teen accounts)

Accounts registered as 13–17 can only be messaged directly by accounts they follow. This applies to 1:1 direct messages.

Sensitive content filtering

Content flagged as sensitive is hidden behind a warning label instead of shown outright. This is on by default for teen accounts and cannot be turned off by a linked guardian.

Teen Accounts & Family Center

What teen accounts get by default

Accounts registered as 13–17 automatically get: a private account, restricted messaging, sensitive content filtering, and a daily time limit with break reminders. See our Terms of Service for full detail.

Linking a guardian

From Settings → Screen Time & Safety, a teen can invite a parent or guardian. Once linked, the guardian can view screen time and adjust the time limit, break reminders, messaging permission, and whether the account is public or private, from Family Center. Sensitive-content filtering always stays on, for everyone. A teen can always switch their own account back to private; only the linked guardian can make it public again.

Unlinking a guardian

Either the teen or the linked guardian can end the link at any time from the same Screen Time & Safety screen. Doing so resets the account's safety settings back to their protective defaults, and lets the guardian know.

Language & Translation

Changing the app's display language

Go to Settings → Language to choose from the languages Loopnod supports. This changes menus, buttons, and system text throughout the app.

Translating a caption

If a Loop's caption is in a different language, tap Translate beneath it to see a translated version. This sends only that caption's text — never your messages or any other content — to our translation service. See our Privacy Policy for detail.

Account & Settings

Changing your username

Settings → Edit Profile → Username. Limited to once every 30 days.

Managing devices

Settings → Linked Devices shows every device signed into your account. Linking a new device brings over your past conversation history from one of your other active devices, so it doesn't start out empty. Remove a device you no longer use or don't recognize from there — this also revokes that device's ability to decrypt future messages.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete Account. This is permanent; see our Privacy Policy for what happens to your data afterward.

Music

Browsing and playing tracks

Open the Music tab to search and browse uploaded tracks. Tap a track to start playback; use the mini-player at the bottom to control playback from anywhere in the app.

Uploading a track

From Music → Upload, select an audio file and add title, artist, and cover art. Uploaded tracks are public content, subject to the same Community Standards as Loops. You can turn on "Allow offline download" so listeners can save the track for offline listening.

Downloading a track for offline listening

If the artist has allowed it, you'll see a Save Offline option from Now Playing. Downloaded audio is stored only on your device, in a location other apps can't open, and isn't available for tracks the artist hasn't enabled downloads for.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Loopnod free? Does it show ads?

Loopnod is currently free to use, and we don't show third-party ads or sell your data for ad-targeting. See our Advertising Policy for detail, and our Terms of Service Section 8 if we ever introduce paid features.

Can Loopnod read my direct messages or Group messages?

No. Direct messages and Groups are end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot read their content — not for moderation, not for ads, and not in response to a request, because the technical design of the encryption doesn't give us a way to. See our Privacy Policy Section 1.5.

I reinstalled the app / got a new phone and my messages are gone. Can you recover them?

Only if you had chat backup turned on, or had another linked device to sync from before your old device was lost. Because message encryption keys live only on your device, we have no server-side copy to restore from — this applies to us too, not only to you. See "Backing up your chats" above, and consider turning it on now for next time.

I forgot my end-to-end encrypted backup password. Can you reset it?

No. We never receive, see, or store that password, so there's no "forgot password" flow for an encrypted backup — by you or by us. A standard backup (protected by your regular account sign-in instead) doesn't have this problem, but offers less protection since anyone who can sign into your account can open it.

What's the difference between Block and Mute?

Blocking stops someone from following you, messaging you, or adding you to new Groups, and they aren't told you blocked them. Muting just hides their content from your feed without changing your relationship or notifying them.

How do I get the verified badge?

Apply from Settings → Apply for Verification. Review generally looks at whether the account is authentic and who it says it is, not your follower count. See "Verification" under Getting Started above.

Why isn't my feed showing "recommended" posts from accounts I don't follow?

By design. Loops, Notes, and Status appear in follow-based, reverse-chronological order — we don't run an engagement-optimizing ranking algorithm or inject algorithmically-recommended content from accounts you don't follow.

Why does my (or my teen's) account have messaging and visibility restrictions?

Accounts registered as ages 13–17 get extra protections by default: private account, restricted messaging, sensitive-content filtering, and a daily time limit. See "Teen Accounts & Family Center" above and our Terms of Service for full detail. These lift automatically once the account turns 18.

How do I report copyright infringement?

Email loopnod.support@gmail.com with "DMCA" or "Copyright" in the subject line. See our Terms of Service Section 10.2 for what to include. Uploaded music tracks also go through an automated copyright check before becoming fully available.

I think a moderation action against my account was a mistake. Can I appeal?

Yes — use the in-app reporting/appeal flow where the action was applied, or email loopnod.support@gmail.com describing what happened and why you believe it was in error. We'll generally explain the reason for an action where we can do so without compromising an investigation.

What happens to my data when I delete my account?

We remove your content and account data from active systems and complete removal from our internal backups/caches within a reasonable period, except where the law requires us to keep something (for example, records tied to a CSAM report). Chat backups you saved to your own Google Drive aren't ours to delete — manage or remove those separately from your Google Drive or from Settings before deleting your account. See our Privacy Policy Section 5.

How do I know if someone I'm messaging is really who I think they are?

Open the chat, tap their name, and use Safety Number to verify. This protects against a compromised key exchange in a way that encryption alone can't catch — see "Verifying a safety number" above.

Troubleshooting

Not receiving notifications

Check that notifications are enabled both in your device's system settings and in-app under Settings → Notifications.

Playback stops in the background

Make sure background app refresh / background activity is allowed for Loopnod in your device settings — some devices' battery optimization features can pause audio playback when the app isn't in the foreground.
