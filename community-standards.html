Community Standards

What's allowed on Loopnod, and how we enforce it.

Effective Date: September 4, 2026 · Version: 2026-09-04

These Community Standards explain what's allowed on Loopnod — in Loops, Notes, Status posts, profiles, Groups, and direct messages — and how we enforce them. They're incorporated by reference into our Terms of Service. Public content (Loops, Notes, Status, profiles) is reviewed against these Standards through user reports, automated detection, and human review. Direct messages and Groups are end-to-end encrypted, so we rely primarily on reports from participants for those — see "How we review messages" below.

1. Safety

Child safety

We have zero tolerance for content that sexually exploits or endangers a minor, in any form, including AI-generated or fictional depictions. This includes grooming behavior even where it doesn't rise to the level of exploitative imagery. Violations result in immediate, permanent account termination and a mandatory report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as required by U.S. federal law.

Harassment, bullying & threats

Don't target another person with repeated unwanted contact, intimidation, threats, or content designed to demean or degrade them. This applies across public posts, Groups, and direct messages.

Self-harm, suicide & eating disorders

We don't allow content that promotes or facilitates suicide, self-harm, or eating disorders. Genuine recovery support, awareness content, and personal experience shared in a non-promotional way are allowed.

Violent extremism

We don't allow content that threatens, incites, promotes, or facilitates terrorism or violent extremism, or that glorifies violent extremist organizations or individuals.

Human trafficking & exploitation

We don't allow content that facilitates human trafficking or exploitation of any kind.

2. Respectful Behavior

Hate speech

We don't allow content that discriminates against or incites hatred toward individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, or veteran status.

Non-consensual intimate imagery

We don't allow sharing intimate images or video of someone without their consent, regardless of how the content was originally obtained, regardless of whether the image is real, and regardless of whether it was digitally altered or AI-generated. A sexual or intimate "deepfake" of a real person is treated the same as a real photo for purposes of this rule.

Doxxing

Don't post another person's private information — home address, phone number, financial information, government ID, or similar — without their consent.

Impersonation

Don't create an account, profile, or piece of content designed to impersonate another person or entity or deceive others about who you are. Parody and commentary accounts are fine as long as they're not designed to mislead.

3. Groups

Groups are a shared space, and the person who adds you controls that first step — being added to a Group doesn't require your advance approval, similar to most group-messaging products. From there:

  • Add people in good faith. Only add someone to a Group if you reasonably believe they want to be there. Repeatedly re-adding someone who has left, or using Group invitations to get around a block, is treated as harassment.
  • Admins set the tone. Group admins can add or remove members, promote or demote other admins, and optionally restrict posting to admins only. Use these controls fairly — using them to retaliate against or silence a member in bad faith is a violation.
  • You can always leave. Any member can leave a Group at any time, regardless of what an admin has restricted.
  • Report and block work the same as everywhere else. If a Group or a member in it violates these Standards, you can leave, block the member, and report it to us.

4. Platform Integrity

Spam & fake engagement

Don't send bulk unsolicited messages, run fraudulent schemes, solicit for pyramid or multi-level-marketing schemes, or use bots/scripts/fake accounts to inflate followers, likes, or other engagement metrics. To help keep this in check, the Service applies automated limits on how quickly you can post, follow, or message in a short period — you'll see a brief message if you hit one.

Synthetic & manipulated media

Don't share manipulated or synthetically-generated media — including deepfakes — presented as authentic where it's reasonably likely to deceive people or cause harm, such as fabricated audio or video of a real person, or content designed to mislead about an election or a public health emergency. Clearly-labeled satire, parody, and creative work are fine.

Security

Don't attempt unauthorized access to any account or system, distribute malware, scrape or access the Service using automated means without our prior written permission, or attempt to intercept, tamper with, or circumvent the encryption used for direct messages and Group messages.

One account per person

Multiple accounts used to evade a suspension, manipulate engagement, or otherwise get around these Standards are not allowed.

5. Intellectual Property

Don't post content that infringes someone else's copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights. Music tracks are automatically checked for a match against known copyrighted recordings and may be restricted if flagged; see our Terms of Service Section 10.3. If you believe your copyright has been infringed, see the DMCA process in the same Section.

6. How We Review Content & Messages

Public content (Loops, Notes, Status posts, profiles) can be reviewed by us directly — through reports, automated tools, and human moderators — because it isn't end-to-end encrypted.

Direct messages and Group messages are end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot read their content. Any participant can report something they've personally received, since they can see it decrypted on their own device; from there, we can act on the report, on message/Group metadata, and on patterns across multiple reports, even without ourselves having read the underlying content.

7. Enforcement

Depending on the severity and pattern of a violation, we may: leave content up with a warning label; reduce its visibility; remove it; issue a warning to the account; temporarily restrict features (like posting or messaging); or suspend or permanently terminate the account. Severe violations, including CSAM and credible threats of violence, result in immediate termination and, where legally required, a report to law enforcement or NCMEC — without warning.

We'll generally let you know the reason for an enforcement action where we can do so without compromising an investigation or violating a legal obligation. If you believe an enforcement action was made in error, you can appeal it through the in-app reporting flow or by contacting us at loopnod.support@gmail.com.

8. Reporting Tools

Use the in-app Report and Block/Mute tools whenever you encounter content or behavior that violates these Standards. Report in good faith — don't submit false or malicious reports to harass another user or suppress content you simply disagree with.
